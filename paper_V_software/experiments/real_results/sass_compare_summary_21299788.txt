Job 21299788: SASS instruction category comparison, fused_gpu_bench.c
(C) vs fused_gpu_bench.f90 (Fortran), extracted via cuobjdump.

Investigates: why Fortran runs faster than C on GPU (1.45-2.81x,
Paper IV Section 6.2) despite C running faster than Fortran on CPU
for the same denotational computation (1.2-1.85x, Section 6.1).

Result: HONESTLY INCONCLUSIVE. Fortran generates MORE instructions
in every category, not fewer:

           C      Fortran   diff
total:    3720    4144     +424 (+11.4%)
FFMA/DFMA: 520     538      +18  (+3.5%)
LDG/STG:   392     400       +8  (+2.0%)
LDS/STS:    40      48       +8 (+20.0%)
branches:  354     403      +49 (+13.8%)

This rules out a naive "fewer instructions = faster" explanation
(Fortran has more of everything yet runs faster), but does not
provide a positive explanation. Raw SASS instruction-category counts
don't capture scheduling, latency-hiding, or memory-coalescing
quality -- any of which could explain more-instructions-but-faster.

to see WHERE cycles are actually spent, not just what instructions
exist.
